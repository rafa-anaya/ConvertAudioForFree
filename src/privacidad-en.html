Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 20, 2026

At AudioTool Pro (referred to as "the Site" or "the Tool"), our primary priority is safeguarding your privacy and ensuring a fully secure environment for handling your files.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Absolute Privacy)

Our application is designed under a Client-Side and Serverless architecture (no backend server). This means that none of your uploaded audio, music, or video files are transmitted over the internet or uploaded to our servers. All encoding, extraction, and trimming are performed via the computing capabilities of your own web browser and physical device using local WebAssembly technologies. We do not have access to, nor do we store, any portion of the files you work with here.

2. Use of First-Party Cookies

This tool saves minimal required settings using your browser's LocalStorage (for example, if you previously chose a format or interface options) solely for the purpose of use during your sessions, which does not share data outside your device.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep the tool free, we display advertising operated by third-party networks, primarily through our monetization partner Google AdSense. Therefore, you should be aware of the following:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • The use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads tailored to our users based on their browsing.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or alternatively, by choosing to opt out or manage such services through groups like www.aboutads.info.

4. Minors

AudioTool Pro is a universal tool that does not require profile registration. We do not capture filial or identity data from individuals, making its use general and anonymous.

5. Changes to these policies

We reserve the right to update this document as applicable to new global legal directives (GDPR, CCPA) while maintaining the spirit of privacy at its core. We suggest visiting this page occasionally.
