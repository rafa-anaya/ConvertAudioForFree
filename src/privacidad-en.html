Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last Updated: April 22, 2026

1. General Information and Contact

Welcome to AudioTool Pro. We take your privacy very seriously. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach out to us through the contact section or by sending a direct message.

2. File Processing (Local Technology)

AudioTool Pro runs strictly on the client-side (in your own browser). This means that:

  • Zero storage: No files, documents, or data you input are ever uploaded to our servers.
  • Local processing: All audio and video processing happens directly on your device using WebAssembly technology.
  • Total privacy: We do not store, share, or have any access to the files you process.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense as a third-party vendor to show advertisements and fund this free project.

  • Use of cookies: Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized ads: These cookies enable Google and its partners to serve personalized advertising tailored to your interests.
  • Opt-out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or the AboutAds.info portal.

4. Donations and Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to third-party platforms (such as PayPal or Ko-fi) to receive voluntary donations.

  • Zero financial data: Our website does not process, request, or have access to bank details, credit cards, or billing information.
  • Third-party responsibility: By clicking a donation button or an external link, you leave our website. From that moment, your browsing is subject to the terms and privacy policies of that external payment platform.

5. International Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Although we do not collect personal data directly, we respect the privacy rights of international users, including residents of the European Economic Area (GDPR) and California (CCPA).

  • Cookie control: You have full control to accept, reject, or revoke your consent to advertising cookies at any time through our website's privacy banner or through your own browser settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not intentionally collect or solicit personal information or any kind of data from minors.
